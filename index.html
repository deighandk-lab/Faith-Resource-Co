About Faith Resource Co.

Faith Resource Co. was established with a passion for making Christian resources, educational material, and faith-based inspiration more accessible to individuals, families, churches, ministries, and communities across South Africa.

Our Mission

To provide meaningful faith-based resources that encourage spiritual growth, learning, creativity, and purpose.

Our Vision

To become a trusted Christian online resource hub that impacts lives through knowledge, faith, and community support.

Our Values

Faith, integrity, service, excellence, compassion, and community remain at the heart of everything we do.

Our Story

Formerly known as Faith Resource Centre, Faith Resource Co. represents the continuation of a meaningful legacy built on faith, service, and community impact. As the brand evolves into a modern online platform, our commitment remains the same — to inspire lives, strengthen faith, and provide valuable resources that make a lasting difference.
