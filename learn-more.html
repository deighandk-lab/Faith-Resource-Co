Our Story

Formerly known as Faith Resource Centre, Faith Resource Co. represents the continuation of a meaningful vision rooted in faith, community, and knowledge.

What began as a trusted Christian resource store has evolved into a modern online platform focused on providing books, devotionals, stationery, and faith-inspired resources to individuals, ministries, schools, and churches nationwide.

Through growth and renewal, our mission remains the same: to inspire lives, strengthen faith, and equip communities with resources that make a lasting impact.
